# 🛡️ YatraShield

### A Smart Tourism Platform for Safer, Smarter & More Personalized Travel Across India

YatraShield is a smart tourism platform designed to help travelers make safer and more informed decisions while exploring India.

The platform brings together **tourist safety information, risk awareness, destination discovery, emergency assistance, and personalized travel support** in one unified interface.

---

## 🌟 Why YatraShield?

Travelers often have to rely on scattered information when planning trips or dealing with unexpected situations.

YatraShield aims to simplify this experience by providing:

- 🗺️ **Tourism & Destination Discovery**
- 🛡️ **Tourist Safety & Risk Awareness**
- 🚨 **Emergency Assistance**
- 📍 **Location-based Safety Information**
- 🧭 **Personalized Travel Support**
- 📊 **Risk Visualization**
- 🏛️ **India-focused Tourism Information**

The goal is to make travel **safer, smarter, and more convenient** through a single digital platform.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🗺️ Interactive Risk Map
Visualize tourism-related risk information across different locations and identify areas requiring additional awareness or caution.

### 🛡️ Safety & Risk Awareness
Provides travelers with relevant safety information to help them make better-informed travel decisions.

### 🚨 Emergency Support
Quick access to important emergency assistance and safety-related information when travelers need it most.

### 🧭 Smart Travel Experience
Helps users explore destinations while keeping safety and travel context in consideration.

### 📍 Location-Based Information
Provides tourism and safety information relevant to the selected destination.

### 🇮🇳 India-Focused Tourism
Designed specifically around travel experiences and destinations across India.
